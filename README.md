# Sales Enquiry & Offer Management Dashboard

## Project Overview
Developed an Excel-based Sales Enquiry, Offer & Clarification Management System to streamline enquiry tracking, quotation follow-ups, pending clarification monitoring, and sales performance reporting in a manufacturing environment.

The project consolidates domestic and export enquiries, customer clarifications, and offer-related information into a centralized reporting system. It provides management with better visibility into enquiry status, pending tasks, overdue items, and monthly business performance through interactive dashboards and MIS reports.

## Key Features

- **Centralized Enquiry Management:** Maintains domestic and export enquiries across different product categories, including FRP equipment, pipes, fittings, gratings, and infrastructure.
- **Offer & Clarification Tracking:** Tracks customer enquiries, offer details, clarification requests, due dates, and current status.
- **Automated Follow-up Monitoring:** Consolidates pending items into a single follow-up sheet and identifies overdue enquiries.
- **Priority Management:** Categorizes enquiries based on priority levels (P1, P2, P3).
- **MIS Dashboard:** Provides a consolidated overview of total enquiries, pending, completed, regret, and hold cases.
- **Monthly Performance Reporting:** Analyzes monthly enquiry volume, completion status, regret percentage, and clarification trends.
- **Client-wise Analysis:** Helps identify customers with pending enquiries and monitor follow-up requirements.

## Business Objectives
- Improve enquiry and quotation tracking.
- Reduce delays in customer follow-ups.
- Monitor pending and overdue activities.
- Improve visibility into sales pipeline performance.
- Support management decision-making through MIS reporting.

## Project Outcome
Created a centralized reporting solution that brings enquiry management, clarification tracking, follow-up monitoring, and monthly performance analysis together in one Excel workbook.

This project demonstrates practical applications of Excel, data analysis, MIS reporting, and business process management in a manufacturing sales environment.
